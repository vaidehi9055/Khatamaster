# 📊 Khata Master: Udhar Hisab Book

Khata Master is a smart and simple digital ledger app designed for businesses and individuals to manage credit, debit, and Show Your profit & loss easily.

---

# 📱 App Overview

Khata Master helps users manage their daily business transactions digitally and securely.

- Manage Customer Accounts
- Track Credit & Debit
- Smart Transaction History
- Business Friendly UI

---

# ✨ Features

- 📒 Digital Khata Book
- ➕ Add Customer
- 💰 Credit & Debit Entry
- 📊 Transaction History
- 🔍 Search Customers
- 📅 Date-wise Records
- 📱 Simple & Clean UI
- 🔐 Secure Data
- ⚡ Fast Performance
