# 📊 Khata Master - Digital Khata Book & Udhar Manager

Khata Master is a simple yet powerful digital khata book app that helps businesses manage udhar, credit, debit, and daily transactions easily.

Whether you run a small business, shop, trade, or work as a freelancer, Khata Master helps you move from traditional paper khata to a smart digital ledger.

---

# 📱 App Overview

Khata Master helps you:

- Track customer accounts
- Manage udhar hisab
- Record credit and debit entries
- Monitor daily business transactions
- Maintain digital business ledger

No more paper records. No more confusion.  
Everything in one digital khata book.

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 📒 Digital Khata Book
Convert your traditional khata book into a smart digital ledger.

## 💰 Udhar Hisab Management
Track credit, debit, and udhar accounts easily.

## 👥 Customer Ledger Accounts
Create separate ledger accounts for each customer.

## 📊 Daily Transaction Tracking
Record income and expenses for your business.

## ⚡ Simple & Easy to Use
Clean and user-friendly interface.

## 🔐 Secure Data Storage
Your business data remains safe and secure.

## 📱 Access Anytime
Check your business accounts anytime from your phone.
