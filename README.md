## 📖 About

**Khata Master** replaces the paper khata book with a smart digital ledger. It helps shop owners
track udhar (credit), manage customers **and** suppliers, keep stock under control, create GST bills
and back up everything safely to Google Drive.

The app has five tabs on the bottom navigation bar:

| 👥 Customers | 🛍️ Purchase | 📦 Inventory | 🧾 History | 💳 Billing |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Udhar ledger for every customer | Suppliers and purchase entries | Stock, price, GST and alerts | Invoice summary for everyone | Create and share PDF bills |

---

## 🆕 What's New in v1.9

- 🛍️ **Suppliers & Purchase**: a new *Purchase* tab with a supplier list, supplier balance and *New Purchase* entries
- 📅 **Today's Summary**: daily cash summary with **Money In**, **Money Out** and **Net Today**, for customers and suppliers
- 🌐 **10 languages** for number-to-word amounts: English, हिन्दी, ગુજરાતી, मराठी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, ಕನ್ನಡ, বাংলা, ଓଡ଼ିଆ, اردو
- 🔔 **Low-stock notifications**: e.g. *"Rice is running low! Stock: 2 pcs"*
- ☁️ **Backup & Restore**: back up to **Google Drive** or export a local `.db` file, and restore on any phone
- 🔐 **App Lock** with fingerprint or phone lock (Premium)
- 📱 **Home screen widget** for a quick look at your khata
- 📊 **More reports**: Customer List, Supplier List, Contact List, Collection, Daily Collection and all-time Transaction reports
- 💹 **Profit & Collection (Today)** with gross profit
- 🧾 **Itemized billing**: transactions show the items sold, e.g. *Cream Chocolate (2 pcs), Rice (2 kg)*
- 🏷️ **Business logo and UPI ID** on your invoices
- 🧮 **GST Calculator** built in
- 👑 **Premium subscription** via Google Play

## 🔄 How It Works

```
1. Add customers & suppliers  →  name, address, mobile number
2. Record transactions        →  Receive (In) / Send (Out) with a remark
3. Add inventory items        →  price, stock, GST, low-stock alert
4. Record purchases           →  select supplier, add items and bill number
5. Create a bill              →  choose customer → select items → generate PDF
6. Check today's summary      →  Money In, Money Out, Net Today, profit
7. Back up                    →  Google Drive or local file
```
